About FNC

Fight Nation Championship

FNC is a Croatian mixed martial arts promotion that has quickly grown into one of the leading MMA organizations in the region, staging events across Croatia and neighbouring countries.

This site is an independent fan project that turns FNC results into data — so fans, analysts and fighters can see trends that are invisible from the cageside seat.
